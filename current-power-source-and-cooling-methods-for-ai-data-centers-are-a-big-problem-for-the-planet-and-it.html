Core Insight

No perpetual motion. Magnets don't create energy — they convert it with far less friction and loss. The real leverage point is cooling. Removing heat without water or chillers cuts 30–60% of a data center's parasitic load.

Solution Pillars

Power

Permanent-magnet direct-drive wind/solar + magnetic-bearing flywheels for ride-through. SMR or geothermal option for base load. Zero on-site combustion, zero diesel.

Cooling

Direct-to-chip two-phase dielectric (PG25) then full immersion. Closed loop, WUE ~0, PUE 1.05–1.1. 30–60% cooling energy cut vs. air.

Architecture

Designed for 50–100kW racks. Heat reuse for building HVAC and greenhouse. Sensor-rich ops with real-time PUE/WUE telemetry.

Pilot Plan

Phase 1 6 MONTHS

  • 2 racks, 80–150kW total load
  • Budget: $250k (cooling skids, retrofit, sensors)
  • Metrics: PUE, WUE, kWh saved, water gallons avoided

Phase 2 SCALE

  • Expand to 1MW pod in Carrollton
  • Add flywheel + behind-meter solar
  • Target: PUE ≤1.08, WUE = 0, payback <3yr

Why Now

Vendors are already shipping: Vertiv CoolPhase Flex, ZutaCore HyperCool, Schneider Uniflair XCA. Industry is moving from air to liquid at 50kW+ per rack. Texas SB-6 and water restrictions make zero-water designs a competitive advantage.
